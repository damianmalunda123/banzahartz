The Artist Behind The Pen

Baraka Banzah

I am Baraka Banzah, an independent professional drawing artist based in Tanzania. I dedicate my life to the classical discipline of hand-drawn portraiture, hyper-detailed graphite shading, and intricate ballpoint pen artwork.

"A drawing doesn't just replicate light — it reconstructs humanity. When you place thousands of individual strokes on virgin paper, you breathe a heartbeat into the image."

My mission is to craft heirlooms that evoke genuine, enduring emotion for clients across the globe, And this is a right place to express your emotional.

Pure Physical Mediums

Tools of the Atelier

Zero digital filters. Zero automated prints. Only premium materials selected for archival longevity.

Graphite & Charcoal

Faber-Castell & Staedtler graphite pencils (2H to 9B) providing smooth gradations from silvery highlights to deep velvety charcoal blacks.

Bic Ballpoint Pens

Fine-tip 0.7mm and medium 1.0mm blue and black ballpoint pens for irreversible, feather-light hatching with unmatched vitality.

Archival Bristol Paper

Heavyweight 250gsm to 300gsm acid-free smooth Bristol and cartridge paper that resists yellowing and preserves contrast for decades.

Tortillons & Fixatives

Custom paper blending stumps, horsehair dry brushes, and museum UV fixative spray protecting against graphite smudges and sunlight.

Creative Vision

Artistic Mastery & Disciplines

Every portrait combines relentless geometric observation with raw emotional depth.

Fine Art Mastery

  • Anatomical Realism: Precise facial geometry, subtle skin creases, and ocular reflections that convey true human emotion.
  • Cross-Hatching Control: Controlled line density that mimics shadow falloff without erasing.
  • Hair & Fabric Texture: Individual strand rendering and tactile cloth fold weights.
  • Emotional Depth: Capturing the unspoken story behind eyes, smiles, and contemplative expressions.

The Studio Standard

  • 100% Traditional Hand-Drawn: Every gradient and hair strand is drawn on physical paper by hand.
  • Archival Durability: Finished with protective sealant to ensure the artwork lasts for generations.
  • Methodical Focus: Devoting 15 to 40 hours of patient, stroke-by-stroke concentration per piece.
  • Worldwide Safe Delivery: Packaged in reinforced protective sleeves and art tubes for secure delivery.

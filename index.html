Tools, Technique & Disciplines

Every artwork is crafted purely with traditional physical instruments — no digital filters or AI shortcuts.

Hyper-Realistic Graphite

Utilizing graded pencils from 2H to 9B, layered with tortillons and dry brushing to recreate photorealistic skin pores, gentle light falloffs, and hair textures that look alive.

Intricate Ballpoint Pen

A discipline where mistake is not an option. Millions of controlled ink strokes and cross-hatches weave together to produce breathtaking depth, character, and permanence.

Custom Memorials & Gifts

Turning beloved wedding photographs, ancestral portraits, or iconic sports heroes into heirloom artwork that will hang proudly on living room walls for generations.

Seamless Process

How To Commission Your Portrait

Getting your custom drawing made and delivered is simple and completely transparent.

01

Send Reference Photo

Send your clearest, favorite picture via WhatsApp or Email. We will discuss composition, lighting, and focus.

02

Select Medium & Size

Choose between graphite pencil or ballpoint pen, and select your preferred dimensions (A4, A3, or massive A2).

03

Crafting & Updates

I sketch and detail your artwork stroke-by-stroke. You receive behind-the-scenes progress photos along the way.

04

Fixative & Delivery

Treated with professional UV art fixative, safely packaged in rigid framing/tubes, and delivered to your doorstep.

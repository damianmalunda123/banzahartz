Common Questions

Commission FAQs

How long does a portrait take to complete?

Standard portraits (A4 and A3) are completed within 3 to 7 business days. Rush orders can be accommodated upon request depending on studio schedule.

What photo should I provide?

High-resolution photos with good lighting and clear eye/facial details produce the most breathtaking results. You can send multiple photos via WhatsApp, and I will help select the best reference.

How is the drawing protected during shipping?

Every drawing is sprayed with museum-grade UV fixative spray to seal the graphite or ink, wrapped in acid-free tissue paper, and sandwiched between rigid boards or rolled inside a reinforced art tube.

Do you ship outside Tanzania?

Yes! We arrange safe courier and international parcel delivery so clients worldwide can enjoy an authentic hand-drawn African portrait.
